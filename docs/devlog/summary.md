# Devlog Summary

- 2026-11-26: **Roads and trains run, and the airport has night traffic** — about 1,700 vehicles at the morning peak on US-101, I-380 and the terminal roads, at 53 to 62 mph on the freeways with nothing standing over three minutes; 1,100 drop-offs and 1,000 pick-ups at the curbs in two hours. Northbound US-101 had no way off the map until ends of the network were told by where a link leads. AirTrain runs both lines, BART and Caltrain run; trains on a loop had been driving past their stops. Cars, trucks and aircraft carry their lights at night. Errands are not yet tied to passengers.
- 2026-11-26: **Aircraft fly the day but do not yet keep apart on the ground** — 589 visits a day land, park, turn round and leave from 91 usable gates; the new benchmark `tools/sim/soak.ts` fails on all five seeds: fuselages meet dozens of times a day, and two seeds still lock up. More than five repairs did not converge, so a hard-problem search is open in the [plan](../work/0_sfo-simulation/plan.md), one branch continuing.

- 2026-11-25: **The airport stands in 3D, by day and by night** — runways with their markings, taxiways, 104 gates with jet bridges, terminals, garages, the elevated roads and the AirTrain guideway are built from the layout into about 460,000 triangles and draw at 60 fps on the GPU at 1920×1080. The sun follows the clock; at night the airfield lights, apron floodlights and street lamps come up. Nothing moves yet. [Plan](../work/0_sfo-simulation/plan.md).
- 2026-11-25: **The real SFO is in the repo as six layout files** — built from OpenStreetMap: 4 runways within 1% of their published lengths, 1,207 taxi nodes, 221 stands, 324 mapped rooms inside the terminals, 2,842 road edges with 115 signals, AirTrain, BART and Caltrain. Two things the map gets wrong were fixed in the build, not by hand: 18 pairs of taxi nodes a few centimetres apart, and 5 buildings drawn twice.
